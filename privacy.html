Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026

Scope

gbrain is a private, single-user application operated for its operator's own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Google data accessed

With the operator's consent, gbrain reads Gmail messages and metadata, Google Calendar events and attendee information, Google Contacts, and the account's primary email identifier. Access is read-only. The application does not request Google Drive access and cannot send email, modify calendars, or edit contacts.

How data is used

Google data is used only to build the operator's private searchable knowledge index, identify relevant context, and surface commitments or follow-ups. Before indexing Gmail threads, structural rules always exclude Promotions and Social messages, mailing-list messages, calendar-system messages, and automated notifications. Among the remaining messages, selection is limited to recent owner-sent mail, starred or important mail, direct mail from a known person, and substantive exchanges with at least three replies. Selected Google data may be processed by an AI service configured by the operator solely to provide this operator with personalized search, summaries, commitment extraction, and related knowledge-assistant features.

Storage, sharing, and retention

Derived data is stored in systems controlled by the operator, including a local computer and private databases or repositories. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, shared with data brokers, or disclosed to unrelated third parties. Service providers process data only as directed by the operator to run the application. Google user data is not used to create, train, or improve generalized machine-learning or artificial-intelligence models. Derived records are retained until the operator deletes them.

Security and access

OAuth credentials are stored in private, access-restricted local storage. Application access is limited to the operator's authenticated devices and accounts.

Google API Limited Use

gbrain's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revocation and deletion

The operator may revoke access at any time from Google Account security settings. Revoking access or disconnecting an account stops future access and removes the stored OAuth authorization, but it does not automatically delete previously derived records; the operator may delete those records from the private knowledge system separately.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to the user-support address displayed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
