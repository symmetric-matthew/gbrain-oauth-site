Private · single-user · read-only

gbrain

gbrain is a private information assistant operated for one user's own Google accounts. It connects read-only to Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Contacts to create a searchable personal knowledge index and surface useful follow-ups.

What it accesses

  • Gmail messages and metadata
  • Calendar events and attendees
  • Contacts and email addresses

The application cannot send email, modify calendars, or edit contacts. Google Drive access is not requested.

Who can use it

This is a private, single-user application. It is not offered as a public service and does not accept outside users.
